06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

Working in another country is a big decision. Here are answers to some things candidates often ask.

Still have a question?We're happy to hear from you.Chat with our team
Can I apply without previous Gulf experience?+
You can register your interest as a first-time candidate. The employer will decide the final experience and eligibility requirements for each available role.
Which countries do you recruit for?+
We focus on domestic and household support opportunities in Saudi Arabia and Qatar. Ask the team about confirmed vacancies, as availability can change.
How much could I earn in Saudi Arabia?+
Our supplied recruitment materials show estimates starting at SAR 900 per month for new contracts and SAR 1,000–1,200 for experienced or returning workers. These are indicative only; the signed offer determines actual pay. Qatar pay will need to be confirmed separately.
What documents will I need?+
Requirements may include a valid passport or ID, proof of identity, medical clearance and other employer or immigration documents. Please do not send sensitive documents through the general enquiry form; ask for the approved secure document process.
Do I have to pay any recruitment fees?+
Ask the agency to give you a written, itemised explanation of any charges and who is legally responsible for them. Do not make payments without verifying the agency, official payee and applicable Kenyan and destination-country rules.
Is a job or visa guaranteed once I apply?+
No. Registration is only an expression of interest. Job placement depends on a confirmed vacancy, employer selection, required documentation and immigration approvals.
How long does the process take?+
There is no fixed timeline. The process depends on the vacancy, interview dates, document checks, medical requirements, visa processing and approvals. Ask for a realistic estimate after an employer is identified.
