CANDIDATE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

How information shared through this website is handled, and what to know before sending personal details.

Website launch note: This is a practical draft reflecting the current website functionality. The company should review and approve it before publishing, particularly if the website later adds online uploads, an applicant database, analytics or third-party form services.

1. Who we are

Al Safwa Manpower Limited is a recruitment business based in Ngara, Nairobi, Kenya. You can contact us on +254 755 541 141 or dennisogato325@gmail.com.

2. Information you may choose to send

If you complete the candidate enquiry form, it prepares a message containing your name, phone number, optional email address, preferred destination, experience, role of interest and any optional comments. If you complete the contact form, it prepares a message containing your name, email, enquiry type and message.

3. How the website works

The current website does not submit application or contact form data to a website server and does not create an applicant account or store a candidate database. Instead, it prepares a message in WhatsApp or your email application. You choose whether to send that message. After you send a message, it will be handled through the relevant communications service and the company's normal communications processes.

4. Why the information is used

The information you choose to send may be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss your interest in work, explain relevant recruitment requirements and communicate about a potential role. Sharing information does not guarantee an interview, employment offer, placement or visa.

5. Other services and external links

The website links to WhatsApp and may open your own email software. These services operate under their own privacy terms. The website also uses Google Fonts, which may involve font requests to Google's servers. No analytics or marketing-cookie scripts are included in this supplied website.

6. Sensitive documents

Please do not enter passport numbers, national identification numbers, medical records, bank information or upload personal documents through these general enquiry fields. If your candidacy progresses, ask the company which secure and lawful channel should be used for sensitive documentation.

7. Retention, access and corrections

For messages you have already sent, contact Al Safwa Manpower Limited to ask how your information is held, whether it can be corrected, or whether you may request deletion under applicable data protection requirements. The company should set and publish its operational retention periods and any disclosures to employers or recruitment partners before collecting further personal information.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice should be reviewed whenever the application process or website technology changes. It describes the website provided in this project and does not replace the company's full internal data protection policy.
