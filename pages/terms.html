IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Website terms.

Helpful terms to understand the information and recruitment enquiries offered through this website.

Website launch note: These are draft informational terms for the supplied website. Al Safwa Manpower Limited should review the language against applicable Kenyan and destination-country recruitment regulations before publishing.

1. About the website

This website introduces Al Safwa Manpower Limited and provides general information about domestic and household support recruitment pathways in Saudi Arabia and Qatar. The content is for general information only and is not legal or immigration advice.

2. Expressions of interest are not job offers

Information about roles on this website is an invitation to enquire. It is not confirmation of an active vacancy, job placement, employer sponsorship, work visa or travel entitlement. Every potential placement depends on a real employer opening, the applicant's eligibility, selection, documented employment terms and official approvals.

3. Salary information

Saudi Arabia salary figures displayed on this website are taken from the company's supplied promotional materials and are estimates only: from SAR 900 per month for new contracts and approximately SAR 1,000–1,200 for some experienced candidates. They may vary by employer, role, experience and contract. Salary information for Qatar must be confirmed by the company and employer. Always rely on a complete written employment offer.

4. Your responsibilities

Provide accurate information in enquiries, review all documents and offers carefully and make sure you understand the duties, wages, time off, accommodation, lawful deductions and other conditions. Do not send sensitive identifying documents in the general form. Seek independent advice or contact relevant authorities if anything is unclear.

5. Recruitment fees and payments

Before making a payment, request a written breakdown of any charges and independently verify that the fees, payee and agency comply with applicable law. Do not send money solely on the basis of an advertisement, social media message or an unverified payment instruction.

6. Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp and email apps are provided for convenience. The website cannot guarantee their availability or delivery of a message, and using them is subject to the provider's terms.

7. Website content

Information may be updated as recruitment needs change. Photos and graphics are illustrative and do not prove a current job opening or particular employer relationship. Any use of the site's branding should be authorised by Al Safwa Manpower Limited.
